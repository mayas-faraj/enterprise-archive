Customer review from Spiceworks .com
Reviewed by:ITCowboy
5 out of 5
Overall Rating: 
Date:July 14, 2011
Review:I purchased a Dell R515 Server as a replacement for an old PowerEdge 4600. This system works amazing well for a Unit costing under $4000. The dual 6 Core Opterons and and 32 Gigs of RAM I ordered with it are sufficient for several virtual machines. You have multiple choices of hardware for this system, but I went with the Hot-Swappable SATA Drives to make it less expensive.
All said I am now running Hyper-V with 3 virtual machines, and will be installing a fourth. The Processing power is only 1-5% used right now, and Ram is hardly being touched (Machines moved to virtual were used to 4 GIGs as they are all 32 bit, but I will be changing that)
With another one of these I will be able to virtualize all of our machines and have redundancy, bringing our total physical server count down to 4, 2 of these in office, 1 remote, and our storage system (yes I have Nic's that work for iSCSI)
I highly reccomend this Server, you cannot beat the power and price combination.
