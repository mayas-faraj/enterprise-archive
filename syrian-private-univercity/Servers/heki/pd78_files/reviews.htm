Title: This machine is a powerhouse!
5 out of 5
Overall Rating: 
Date:March 30, 2010
Review:I helped debug this machine while I was an intern. This machine is AWESOME! The computing power is great, and everything in the machine is easily accessible as well. You don't need tools to take apart the computer. The fan bay can be easily lifted out of the chassis, and there is quick access to the RAM, processors, and the mainboard (motherboard).
The machine is reliable also. The debug engineers continuously slammed and vibrated the prototype to find any weaknesses in the design. We weren't happy with the design unless the prototype was still fully functional after all of the abuse.
All in all, you will be happy with this product.
