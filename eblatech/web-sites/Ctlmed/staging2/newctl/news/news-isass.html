CTL MEDICAL CORPORATION TO SHOWCASE PRODUCT PORTFOLIO AT ISASS 2016 CONFERENCE
By ctlmed | Posted March 30, 2016 | In News

CTL Medical Corporation, a Dallas-based medical device manufacturing and service company, announced today that four members from its executive team will attend the 16th annual International Society of the Advancement of Spine Surgery (ISASS) conference scheduled for April 6-8 in Las Vegas, Nevada. The conference provides CTL Medical Corporation the opportunity to meet spine surgeons and other allied health care professionals from around the world.

CTL Medical Corporation designs, develops and manufactures spinal implants and instruments tailored to fit the unique needs of surgeons and patients. Currently, the company has nine products it will showcase at its trade show booth, number 420.

As a new company, attending the 16th annual ISASS conference is integral because it helps us stay informed and increase our visibility within the industry. In addition, it allows us to network and share our company history with other industry professionals and exhibitors,” said Daniel Chon, president and CEO of CTL Medical Corporation.

  • Andy Choi serves as the chief business development officer and leads all business development efforts for CTL. Choi has over 19 years of industry experience in both startup and large corporate environments, with particular expertise in orthopedics and spine.
  • Rose Moore serves as the vice president of business development and marketing, and is responsible for overseeing the overall management and execution of CTL’s strategic and operational marketing and customer relationship.
  • Dean Fulford serves as the vice president of sales for the northern region and is responsible for overseeing product sales, managing territories and overall procurement process.
  • Lanny Duyck serves as the vice president of sales for the southern region. His strengths include planning and building of businesses, sales teams and regions with distributor-based organizationsFor more information on CTL Medical Corporation, visit http://www.ctlmed.com/. For more information on the ISASS 2016 conference, visit http://www.isass.org/education/conferences/.

About CTL Medical Corporation

CTL Medical Corporation was established in 2015 by Daniel Chon, former president & CEO of AccelSPINE®, with the vision of creating a fully integrated, industry leading, global medical device design, development and manufacturing company. CTL has assembled a world-class executive team, bringing together some of the industry’s most exceptional talent, further extending their position as a leader in medical device design and manufacturing.

About ISASS-International Society for the Advancement of Spine Surgery

The International Society for the Advancement of Spine Surgery is an international, scientific and educational society organized to discuss and assess existing strategies and innovative ideas in the clinical and basic sciences related to spine surgery and to enhance patient care. For more information, visit http://www.isass.org/education/conferences/.
