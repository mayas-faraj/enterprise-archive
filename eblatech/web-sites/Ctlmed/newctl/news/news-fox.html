CTL MEDICAL CORPORATION ACQUIRES RF PRECISION LLC
By ctlmed | Posted January 12, 2016 | In News

CTL Medical Corporation, a Dallas, Texas based medical device manufacturing company, announced today that it has acquired RF Precision, LLC, a Denton, Texas based medical and dental equipment manufacturer. The acquisition went into effect January 1, 2016.

“The acquisition of RF Precision, LLC is our first step in establishing a foundation in manufacturing. By bringing manufacturing in-house, it will allow for improved efficiency as we navigate future acquisitions and intended growth” said Daniel Chon, CEO of CTL Medical Corporation.

RF Precision, LLC was founded in 2003 and serves clients in the medical, fiber optic and electronic industries. RF Precision, LLC is designated as an ISO 13485 and ISO 9001 certified company. These certifications allowed RF Precision, LLC to increase its customer base in North America and expand its services to customers in Europe.

CTL Medical Corporation will retain all current RF Precision, LLC executives and employees, and the plan is to double the manufacturing workforce by early 2017.

About CTL Medical Corporation

CTL Medical Corporation was established in 2015 with the vision of creating an industry leading, global medical device design, development and manufacturing company.

About RF Precision, LLC

RF Precision, LLC was established in 2003. The company specializes in manufacturing precision parts, prototype development and production runs where tight tolerances are critical. RF Precision, LLC serves clients in the medical, fiber optic and electronic industries.
